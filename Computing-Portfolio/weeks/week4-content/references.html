Professional Responsible-AI Guidance

The NIST AI Risk Management Framework, ICO guidance, OECD AI Principles and UNESCO Recommendation provide the principal professional and governance sources used in this portfolio. They were prioritised over vendor principles because they offer independent regulatory, intergovernmental or standards-based perspectives.

Source Evaluation

Academic textbooks provide established explanations of machine learning algorithms and AI concepts. Professional frameworks provide more direct guidance on current governance, privacy and responsible-AI practice.

Vendor guidance is useful for understanding practical tools and organisational approaches, but it should be compared with independent academic, regulatory and professional sources.

Professional-development sources support the SWOT analysis and SMART targets by identifying the importance of continuing learning, evidence of practice and ethical responsibility.
