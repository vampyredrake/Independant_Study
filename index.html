Portfolio Overview

This portfolio documents my independent study undertaken as part of the BSc Computing Level 5 programme. It presents evidence of research, technical analysis, practical understanding and reflective learning across four study themes: web technologies, data structures and algorithms, networking and cyber security, and artificial intelligence with professional development.

The portfolio has been developed as an evolving body of work that demonstrates both technical knowledge and the ability to evaluate computing concepts critically. Each weekly section contains structured notes, comparisons, diagrams, reflections and referenced academic sources.
