Week Overview

Week 3 examines the technologies that allow modern computer systems to communicate, operate through cloud platforms and protect information from cyber threats.

The work considers networking models, internet protocols, cloud service models, virtualisation, security threats, cryptography, access control and the role of the CIA triad in information security.
