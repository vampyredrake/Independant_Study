Week Overview

Week 4 examines artificial intelligence, machine learning, responsible technology and professional development. The focus is not only on how machine learning systems operate, but also on how their design and use can affect individuals, organisations and wider society.

The evidence includes structured academic notes, machine learning comparisons, an AI terminology glossary, a critical ethical discussion, a personal SWOT analysis, three SMART professional-development targets and a supporting reference list.
