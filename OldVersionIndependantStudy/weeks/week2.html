Week Overview

Week 2 examines how data is represented, stored, searched, sorted and analysed. The focus is on selecting suitable data structures and algorithms rather than treating one approach as appropriate for every problem.

The evidence is organised into focused sections covering academic notes, data structure comparisons, search algorithms, Big-O complexity, database approaches, critical reflection and references.
